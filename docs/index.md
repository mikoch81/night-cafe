# Night Café

A night shift behind the counter of a small café: catch the cups before they hit the floor, shoo
Paprika off the stools, keep your footing when Noir gets mischievous, and survive the terrible ten
seconds. A handheld-style arcade game for Android.

- [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mikoch81.nightcafe)
